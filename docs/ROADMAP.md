> 1. **🥇 No documented path to finding client #1 at all (closed this pass).** Every commercial asset in the repo — `PRICING_PACKAGING.md`, `CLIENT_ONBOARDING_INTAKE.md`, `ONBOARDING_SOP.md`, the service agreement, all 12 workflows — picks up at the exact instant `ONBOARDING_SOP.md` Phase 0 begins: *"Trigger: Prospect agrees to move forward."* Nothing in the repo, and nothing on the website (which is built to *convert* visitors who already arrived, not generate them), answers the more basic question: **how does a prospect arrive at "agrees to move forward" in the first place, when there's no inbound traffic, no search ranking, and no published case study yet?** This is the literal bottleneck in front of every other piece of infrastructure — a fully-tested 12-workflow stack and a polished website both sit completely idle without it. > **2026-06-08 — Building Workflow 12, Client ROI Report, AND introducing the "reusable framework" lens (eighth pass, same day):** The user reaffirmed the standing priority order (client support readiness, system-health monitoring, TCPA/compliance, ROI reporting, retention/expansion — closed/closed/closed/**now closed**/ongoing) **and** layered a brand-new strategic clarification on top of it: roofing is "Version 1 of the framework, not the final market" — the long-term goal is a *reusable service-business automation framework* portable to HVAC, plumbing, dental, med spa, legal, and other lead/appointment-driven businesses. The instruction was explicit that this should change *how* future work gets done (favor industry-agnostic patterns in architecture/docs/deployment/monitoring/reporting/ops "whenever practical") without changing *what* gets prioritized first (still: land roofing client #1). This build was the perfect single move to honor both directives at once — it was already next in line as the last remaining named workflow-shaped Open Item, *and* it was the natural place to first apply the new lens, since a client-facing report's language is exactly the kind of thing that either locks a system into one vertical's vocabulary or doesn't. **`12_client_roi_report`** (`ocAnTMCh068BxxXz`) is now live and tested (execution 85): every 30 days it reads Leads/Appointments/Communication Log, computes trailing-window metrics (new leads captured, hot/urgent leads flagged instantly, missed calls recovered via instant text-back, appointments booked vs. kept), and texts the *client* — not an internal digest, but a relationship/retention-framed recap addressed to their own brand name, closing with an open invitation to discuss the math. This is deliberately distinct in *purpose*, not just audience, from workflows 07/08 (which are operational pipeline tools the owner uses to run their business day-to-day) — 12 exists to answer "is this system worth what I'm paying for it," the literal question that determines contract renewal. **Applying the reusability lens concretely:** every metric name, every line of SMS copy, and every constant in the build was chosen to generalize — "leads captured," "appointments booked/kept," "missed calls recovered," "follow-ups sent" are universal service-business vocabulary (a dental practice, an HVAC company, and a law firm all have leads, appointments, missed calls, and follow-ups; none of the copy says "roof," "shingle," or "storm damage"). The cadence (`daysInterval: 30` rather than calendar-month math) was chosen specifically because "every 30 days" is a simpler, edge-case-free, and equally-explainable line to any client regardless of industry. The one new constant introduced, `REPORT_WINDOW_DAYS`, deliberately drives *both* the schedule cadence and the metric-window math from a single source — change the cadence once, and the reported numbers automatically describe the same window, in any clone. And rather than minting a new `CLIENT_PHONE` constant for what is conceptually "the client," the build deliberately reuses the existing `OWNER_PHONE` convention (it's the same recipient as 07/08/10/11 — the business owner *is* the client in this single-owner-operator model that roofing/HVAC/plumbing/dental/legal SMBs overwhelmingly share) — fewer uniquely-named per-client constants means faster, less error-prone cloning. Tested live with hand-crafted pinned data spanning both inside and outside the 30-day window boundary across all three source tabs simultaneously — all five computed metrics (`newLeads: 3`, `hotOrUrgentLeads: 1`, `appointmentsBooked: 3`, `appointmentsKept: 2`, `missedCallsRecovered: 2`) matched hand-verified expectations exactly, and the rendered SMS read naturally. Documented across `README.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `CLIENT_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md` (§3 config catalog incl. the new `REPORT_WINDOW_DAYS` row, §3d schedule table, §4 deployment order, §5 verification checklist, §6 now marks this "done"), and `ONBOARDING_SOP.md` (Phase 6 step 2 + Open Items table, now closed). **Both previously-open workflow-shaped Open Items (system-health monitoring, client-facing ROI report) are now closed** — the highest-leverage *named* gaps toward landing-and-retaining client #1 have all been built, tested, and shipped. What remains per the priority order is ongoing: ordinary retention/expansion-readiness work (Phase 5), and continuing to apply the new reusability lens as a standing consideration on every future build — not a separate project, but a filter on how this one gets built out.

> **2026-06-08 — Building Workflow 11, System Health Monitor (sixth pass):** Continuing the user's named priority order (client support readiness, system-health monitoring, TCPA/compliance — closed, ROI reporting, retention/expansion), built and shipped **`11_system_health_monitor`** (`U6t0b7M6lN8eA1JO`, live and tested — executions 75/76). Original plan was to query n8n's own execution history via the `n8n` node's `resource: 'execution'` operation, but `list_credentials` showed no `n8nApi`-type credential exists in the instance and there's no MCP tool to create one — rather than stopping for that as a blocker, the design was redirected around already-available credentials (Google Sheets + Twilio): the workflow now checks **live CRM data freshness** (are there overdue appointment-reminder or follow-up-sequence rows that should have been actioned by now?) once daily, and texts the *operator* — never the client — if either looks stale. This was judged a strictly better design, not a workaround: it requires zero new credentials, and it catches a larger class of failures than execution-metadata monitoring would (e.g., a workflow that "succeeds" on each run but is quietly misconfigured to skip real rows wouldn't show up in execution history, but would show up as stale CRM data). To avoid "crying wolf," the two freshness checks **exactly mirror** the live date-parsing/threshold logic of the workflows they watch (09's 24h/2h reminder windows, 05's Day-1/3/7 follow-up thresholds — extracted via `get_workflow_details`), each with a conservative buffer (2h/1h and 30h respectively) so an alert only fires once the live workflow's *own* definition of "overdue" has been violated with room to spare. Tested twice live: once with a deliberately-stale lead row (confirmed the alert fires with a correctly-formatted, multi-issue-ready message — execution 75) and once with clean data (confirmed the chain stops naturally at zero items with **no** SMS sent — execution 76, the SDK's documented zero-item-safety pattern, no `IF`-gate needed). Documented across `README.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `CLIENT_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md` (§3 config catalog, §3d schedule table, §4 deployment order, §5 verification checklist, §6 optional-enhancements list now marks this "done"), and `ONBOARDING_SOP.md` (Phase 6 step 1 + Open Items table, now closed). **One workflow-shaped Open Item remains: the client-facing ROI report** — next in line per the user's stated priority order.

### Why Acknowledge-and-Alert (not full self-service rebooking)
Building a true two-way SMS slot-negotiation flow (propose times, parse the customer's reply, re-check availability, confirm) is high-complexity, high-risk-of-confusion over SMS, and low marginal value versus a simpler high-confidence design: instantly acknowledge the customer (so they feel heard and stop worrying about a no-show penalty) **and** instantly alert the owner with full context (name, phone, appointment, request type) so staff can close the loop with a phone call — the channel roofing customers actually expect for scheduling changes. This mirrors the static-template-no-AI precedent (03/05/06/07/08/09): reliable, zero-cost, fast, and impossible to derail with an unexpected reply.

Republished and live-tested.

### Compliance Fix (Completed 2026-06-07 — surfaced by sellability review)
A pre-launch evaluation of the system "as if being sold to a roofing company today" surfaced a real TCPA/carrier-compliance exposure: `Normalize Inbound SMS`'s original cancel-intent regex included the bare word `stop`, so a standalone opt-out text like `"STOP"` would have been misclassified as "cancel my appointment" and triggered an automated reply — the opposite of compliant behavior (an automated system must never auto-reply to, or misclassify, a carrier opt-out signal). Patched live via `update_workflow setNodeParameter` (republished `activeVersionId: 91bb00e6-...`): the node now detects standalone opt-out keywords (`STOP`, `STOPALL`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, `QUIT`, `END`, `CANCEL ALL`, `OPT-OUT`, `REMOVE` — matched only when they constitute the *entire* trimmed message via an anchored regex) and routes them to `intent: 'other'` (silently ignored, zero auto-reply) — a defense-in-depth backstop alongside Twilio's carrier-level Advanced Opt-Out handling. Verified live: execution 67 (`"STOP"` → `intent: 'other'`, no reply) and execution 68 (`"please cancel my roofing appointment, something came up"` → `intent: 'cancel'`, full flow ran correctly — proving "cancel" inside a real sentence is still handled as an appointment request, not an opt-out). Documented in `docs/CLIENT_DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md` §7 as a per-client go-live checklist item.

| Reschedule/Cancel intent classification | Keyword regex matching (no AI) | Matches the static-template-no-AI precedent (03/05/06/07/08/09); "cancel"/"reschedule" intents are linguistically distinct enough that keyword matching is reliable, instant, and free — reserving AI spend for judgment calls (lead scoring) where it earns its cost |
