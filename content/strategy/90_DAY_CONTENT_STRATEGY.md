> **Goal of the first 90 days:** Build trust and authority with local service business owners, plant the core reframe ("this was never a marketing problem"), and drive a steady, measurable trickle of qualified people to the Lead Leak Calculator — without faking a single result and without ever sounding like an AI agency.

---

## 1. Strategic Context

Valfin is pre-case-study. We have one live deployment (a Boston-area roofing company) and no published outcome numbers yet. That shapes everything:

- **We cannot lead with proof.** So we lead with *recognition* (naming the pain so precisely the owner feels seen) and *honesty* (the founding-partner story).
- **Our content's #1 job is trust**, because trust is what converts when you don't yet have a wall of testimonials.
- **The Calculator is our conversion engine.** Most consideration/conversion content drives there, because it turns an abstract worry into the owner's own real number — honestly.

**The funnel we're feeding:**
```
Awareness (they feel the pain)
   → Consideration (they see the fix + run their number on the Calculator)
      → Conversion (they message us / book a discovery call)
```

## 2. Content Pillars (the five lanes)

Every post maps to exactly one pillar. (Defined in the Brand Guidelines §10.)

| Pillar | Name | Funnel role | Target mix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | The Leak (problem awareness) | Awareness | 35% |
| **P2** | The Fix (solution education) | Consideration | 25% |
| **P3** | The Proof & The Promise (trust/honesty) | Consideration → Conversion | 15% |
| **P4** | The Operator's Edge (business growth lessons) | Awareness → Consideration | 15% |
| **P5** | The Founder (behind the brand) | Trust (all stages) | 10% |

**Why this mix:** Heavy on awareness (P1) because we're early and need reach + recognition. P2/P3 convert the people P1 wakes up. P4 earns the "this account is actually useful" follow even from people not ready to buy. P5 humanizes a brand that sells reliability — people buy infrastructure from people they trust.

---

## 3. Awareness vs. Consideration vs. Conversion Mix

| Stage | Share of content | Primary pillars | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | 50% | P1, P4 | "Follow for more" |
| **Consideration** | 35% | P2, P3 | "See your number — Calculator (link in bio)" |
| **Conversion** | 15% | P3, P2 | "Want this set up for your business? Message us." |

Rule: **never run two CTAs in one piece.** One ask. Match it to the stage.

## 4. Monthly Themes

Each month has a spine so content compounds instead of feeling random.

### Month 1 — "The Leak You Can't See"
**Theme goal:** Make the invisible problem visible. Most owners don't know how much they lose to missed calls and slow follow-up. Month 1 names it, sizes it, and makes them feel it.
- Heavy P1 (The Leak) and P4 (Operator's Edge / speed-to-lead lessons).
- Signature piece: a worked example showing how a handful of missed calls a month quietly adds up (clearly labeled as an example, not a Valfin result).
- End-of-month: introduce the Calculator as "stop guessing, see your own number."

### Month 2 — "What Calling Back Faster Actually Looks Like"
**Theme goal:** Now that they feel the leak, show the fix — outcome-first, never tech-first.
- Shift toward P2 (The Fix) and P3 (Proof & Promise).
- Walk through the four stages (Capture → Respond → Follow Up → Book) in plain, owner-language terms, one at a time.
- Introduce the founding-partner story honestly: "We built this inside one real roofing company first."

### Month 3 — "Run Like a Bigger Company"
**Theme goal:** Elevate from "stop the bleeding" to "operate at a higher level." Build aspiration + trust, push gently toward conversion.
- Balance across all five pillars; increase P3 and conversion-stage content.
- More founder content (P5) and operator lessons (P4) — position Valfin as the steady operator who gets it.
- Stronger conversion CTAs as trust has compounded over 60 days.

## 5. Posting Cadence

A realistic cadence for a founder-led operation moving toward automation. Quality over quantity (Operating Principle).

| Platform | Posts / week | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram Reels** | 4 | The core engine. Most original videos start here. |
| **TikTok** | 4 | Same videos, repurposed natively (re-captioned, no IG watermark). |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 3–4 | Same videos, evergreen library that compounds via search. |
| **LinkedIn** | 3 | Text posts + native video adaptations; the B2B credibility channel. |

**Weekly rhythm (template):**
- **Mon** — P4 Operator's Edge (lesson to start the week) → LinkedIn + Reel
- **Tue** — P1 The Leak (pain-point Reel) → IG/TikTok/Shorts
- **Wed** — P2 The Fix (solution education) → IG/TikTok/Shorts + LinkedIn
- **Thu** — P1 or P4 → IG/TikTok/Shorts
- **Fri** — P3 Proof & Promise OR P5 Founder → all platforms
- **(rolling)** — 1 conversion-focused Calculator push woven in each week

**Realistic output:** ~4 original videos/week, each repurposed to 4 platforms + carousel + LinkedIn + (weekly) blog/email = a high-leverage week from a small number of source assets.

## 6. Platform Priorities (and why each)

### 1) Instagram Reels — *primary*
**Why:** Highest-reach short-form format for reaching local service owners and the people around them (spouses, office managers, peers who refer). Strong discovery via Reels feed; easy to run light local awareness later. This is where most original content is born.

### 2) TikTok — *secondary, high-upside*
**Why:** Best organic reach for a new account with no audience. Rewards raw, founder-forward, "talking to camera" content — which fits our honest, human voice. Great for the founder (P5) and pain-point (P1) content. Lowest production polish required.

### 3) LinkedIn — *B2B credibility + reach to owners and referrers*
**Why:** Many local business owners, franchise operators, and adjacent professionals (insurance adjusters, real estate agents, suppliers) are active here. It's where "Revenue Operations Infrastructure" credibility lands without sounding salesy. Text-first lessons (P4) and founder reflections (P5) perform well. Also reaches potential partners and future hires.

### 4) YouTube Shorts — *evergreen compounding library*
**Why:** Shorts get surfaced via search and suggested feeds long after posting. Same vertical videos, but this channel builds a durable, searchable asset library ("roofing missed calls," "follow up with leads," etc.) that keeps working months later — unlike the more ephemeral IG/TikTok feeds.

**Deliberately NOT prioritized in the first 90 days:** Facebook/Meta paid ads (no proof to anchor yet), X/Twitter (wrong audience density), and a podcast (too heavy for current stage). Revisit after the case study lands.

## 7. CTA Strategy

| Funnel stage | CTA wording (calm, never shouty) | Destination |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | "Follow for more on plugging the leaks in your business." | Profile / follow |
| Consideration | "See exactly what slow follow-up is costing you — link in bio." | `valfintech.com/calculator` |
| Conversion | "Want us to set this up for your business? Send a message." | `valfintech.com` → "Talk to us" |

**Rules:**
- One CTA per piece. Always.
- The Calculator ("See My Numbers") is the workhorse — it's honest (their inputs, their number) and it qualifies.
- Conversion CTAs increase in frequency across Month 1 → Month 3 as trust compounds.
- Bio link rotates to match the current campaign (default: Calculator).

## 8. Funnel Strategy (how a stranger becomes a discovery call)

```
STRANGER
  │  sees a P1 "Leak" Reel that names their exact Tuesday-afternoon pain
  ▼
RECOGNIZES THEMSELVES  → follows (Awareness CTA)
  │  over days, sees P2 "Fix" + P4 lessons + P5 founder honesty
  ▼
TRUSTS + IS CURIOUS  → taps "link in bio" → Lead Leak Calculator (Consideration CTA)
  │  enters their real numbers → sees their own monthly loss
  ▼
MOTIVATED  → submits Calculator / messages us (Conversion CTA)
  │  enters Valfin's internal lead pipeline (contact form → n8n → Sheet + alert)
  ▼
DISCOVERY CALL  → founding-partner offer → client #1+
```

The content engine's job is the top three rows. The Calculator and the discovery call (already documented in the Operating Manual) take it from there. **Content is measured by how many qualified people it sends into the Calculator and the contact form — not by vanity views.**

## 9. Metrics That Matter (and the ones that don't)

**Track (true business signal):**
- Calculator visits from social (UTM-tagged bio links)
- Contact-form submissions attributed to content
- Follower growth *within the ICP* (local owners), not raw follower count
- Saves + shares (the truest "this is useful/true" signal for our content)
- Watch-through / retention on the hook (first 3 seconds)

**Mostly ignore (vanity):**
- Raw view counts in isolation
- Likes
- Follower count as a headline number

> Honesty applies internally too: we measure what actually moves the business, and we don't dress up vanity metrics as proof of content ROI. (Mirrors the Operating Manual's proof standard.)

## 10. The 90-Day Success Definition

By Day 90, success looks like:
- A consistent, on-brand presence on all four platforms with a clear, recognizable voice.
- A library of ~45–50 original videos, each repurposed across formats.
- A measurable flow of qualified owners hitting the Calculator from content.
- A repeatable production + repurposing system that increasingly runs with less founder time.
- Zero brand violations: no fabricated proof, no "AI agency" drift, no banned language.

This is the foundation. The case study (landing ~60–90 days after client #1) then unlocks a second gear of proof-driven content.
