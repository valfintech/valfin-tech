## 6. AI Visual Prompts (ChatGPT Pro)
- **Primary recommendation: do NOT use AI for the calculator.** Screen-record the live `valfintech.com/calculator` on your own phone — it's real, honest, and more convincing than any generated UI.
- **Talent shots:** if camera-shy and you need a stand-in: "Photorealistic vertical 9:16 photo of an approachable local business owner in their 30s–40s holding a phone, slight curious smile, plain background, natural light, candid, no text."
- **AVOID:** generating a fake calculator UI (it'll look off and undercuts the honesty), robots, sci-fi.

## 7. Realistic AI-Visual Suggestions
This is the one piece where real screen-capture beats AI outright. Record the Calculator flow on your phone (screen recorder is built into iOS/Android, free). Use clearly illustrative inputs so the on-screen result is obviously an example, not a claim. AI stills only as an optional talent stand-in.

Quick, clean cuts that match the "fast and easy" promise. Sync each cut to the screen-record steps (open → type → result). One small zoom-in on the result number to give it a beat of weight. Nothing slow here — the energy is "this is easy, just do it."
