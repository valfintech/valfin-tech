## 4. Subtitle Formatting Recommendations

These are the spoken-word captions (VO transcript), separate from the on-screen text overlays above. Skip this section entirely for Path B (no VO).

- **Generation:** Use CapCut's Auto Captions on the VO track first, then hand-correct any misheard words.
- **Style:** Bold sans-serif (CapCut default "Bold" or "Classic" caption style), white fill, thin black stroke/outline (~2–3px) for legibility over any background.
- **Size:** ~7–8% of frame height — large enough to read at a glance, not so large it crowds the on-screen text overlays.
- **Position:** Centered horizontally, lower-third of frame (around 75% down) — but **stagger vertically from the on-screen text overlays** (Section 3) so the two never collide. If an overlay is also in the lower third for that shot, move the spoken-word caption to the upper-third for that span instead.
- **Casing:** Sentence case, matching the script exactly — no ALL CAPS (per brand voice rules).
- **Line breaks:** Max 2 lines, ~4–6 words per line. Break at natural phrase boundaries (e.g., "They booked the guy / who picked up first" not "They booked the / guy who picked up first").
- **Timing:** Captions should track the VO 1:1, word-for-word — do not paraphrase or compress.
- **Punctuation:** Keep periods at sentence ends for rhythm; drop the final period on the last line of each caption block for a cleaner look (optional, founder's call).

## 5. Exact CapCut Assembly Order

Work top-to-bottom — this is the literal sequence of actions in CapCut Free.

1. **New project** → 9:16, 1080×1920.
2. **Import media**: the 3 (or 5, if Path B) AI stills, talking-head footage (Path A) or none (Path B), 2 phone B-roll clips, optional calendar-add screen recording, VO audio file (if recorded), music track.
3. **Drop clips on the main timeline in this order**: Shot 1 → Shot 2 → Shot 3 (+ insert Shot 3b B-roll as a short cutaway within it) → Shot 4 → Shot 5 → Shot 6 (end card).
4. **Trim each clip to the durations in Section 2** — talking-head clips: cut on the beat of the VO, never mid-word; hard cuts only between all shots.
5. **Apply Ken Burns (5–8% push-in)** to Shots 2, 3, and 4 (and 1B/5B if Path B) — no zoom on talking-head shots.
6. **Add the 0.3s speed-ramp** at the cut into Shot 4 ("booked the guy who picked up first").
7. **Add VO audio track** (Path A) underneath the timeline, synced to the script timestamps.
8. **Run Auto Captions** on the VO track, then restyle per Section 4 and hand-fix any errors.
9. **Add on-screen text overlays** per Section 3 — new text layer per row, timed to the "appears at / holds until" columns, positioned per the staggering rule in Section 4.
10. **Build the end card** (Shot 6): solid dark background + two-line centered white text per Section 3. Hold for 3 full seconds.
11. **Add music track** on a separate audio layer, duck to ~15% under the VO (or as the primary audio bed for Path B). Use CapCut Free search terms: "minimal corporate calm," "documentary underscore," or "soft inspiring no drums."
12. **Final pass**: confirm no exclamation points anywhere on screen, no AI/robot/futuristic visuals slipped in, captions and overlays don't overlap, hard cuts land on the beat.
13. **Export**: 1080p, 30fps, 9:16 — no IG watermark (export clean for the TikTok re-upload too).
