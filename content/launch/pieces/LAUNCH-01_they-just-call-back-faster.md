## 7. Realistic AI-Visual Suggestions
ChatGPT Pro produces strong still photos but not video. Generate the three stills above, then animate each with a slow Ken Burns push-in (5–8% zoom) in CapCut so they read as motion. Keep faces slightly turned or mid-action — fully posed "stock" faces feel fake. Generate 2–3 variants per scene and pick the most candid.

## 8. Suggested B-roll (shoot on a phone, free)
- A real phone ringing/buzzing on a truck seat or workbench.
- A hand reaching for a phone a second too late.
- A calendar app adding an appointment (screen-record on your own phone).
Real B-roll beats AI for these — they're trivial to capture and feel authentic.

## 12. Transition Recommendations
Hard cuts only — no swipes, spins, or flashy effects. One subtle speed-ramp allowed: a quick 0.3s push into Scene 4 ("booked the guy who picked up first") to land the gut-punch. Cut on the beat of the VO, not mid-word.

## 13. Editing Notes (CapCut Free)
- 9:16, 1080×1920. Auto-captions ON, then hand-fix; restyle to bold white with a thin black stroke, centered, lower third.
- Keep talking-head lines tight — trim breaths and dead air so it never drags.
- Ken Burns on every still. No zoom on talking-head shots.
- Duck music under VO to ~15%. End card holds 3 full seconds so the link registers.

Understated, warm, slightly determined — a steady low pulse, no melody fighting the VO. CapCut Free search terms: "minimal corporate calm," "documentary underscore," "soft inspiring no drums." Avoid anything with a drop, EDM, or hype trap beats. Volume sits under the voice the entire time.
