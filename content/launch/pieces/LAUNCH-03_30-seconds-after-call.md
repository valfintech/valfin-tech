## 17. Primary Instagram Caption
Here's what should happen in the 30 seconds after a customer reaches out — even if both your hands are full:

1. It gets caught. Call, text, or form — nothing slips through, no matter how they tried to reach you.
2. They get a real, friendly reply, in your voice. Not a robotic script that makes them feel like a ticket number.
3. If they don't book right away, it follows up for them — day one, day three, day seven — and stops the moment they book or say no. No pestering.
4. The ones who are ready end up on your calendar. The rest, you never had to chase.

That's the difference between "I hope I got back to everyone" and "I know nothing slipped." Save this one. See how it works — link in bio.
