## 17. Primary Instagram Caption
Your best lead this week called at 7:42 at night. Nobody answered.

You were at dinner, or driving, or just finally off the clock — completely fair, you're one person. It went to voicemail. They didn't leave one (almost nobody does anymore), so they called the next name on the list, and that business picked up.

Here's the part that stays with me: you'll never see that lead in any report. There's no missed-call log you check, no note, nothing. It simply never existed to you. That's the most expensive kind of loss there is — the ones you don't even know happened.

Follow for more on the leaks you can't see.
