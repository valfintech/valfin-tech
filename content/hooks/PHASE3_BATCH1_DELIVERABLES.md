## Round 1 revision — what changed and why

**Feedback driving this round:** the original Higgsfield-only renders hallucinated business-critical information — glitching balance figures, garbled counters, unreadable end-card text, an obviously-AI background, and a sideways/rendering-error split screen on HOOK-03. For Valfin, clarity matters more than spectacle.

**General fix applied to all three:** any number, label, UI element, notification, chart, or text that carries the business message is now rendered as a **deterministic Remotion overlay** (Motion Engine components: `KineticText`, custom counters, `GradientBackground`, hand-built cards/icons) — never generated by Higgsfield. Higgsfield's existing footage was repurposed only as cinematic texture, never as the carrier of readable information.

- **HOOK-01 (Draining Balance Timer):** Original `video_raw.mp4` reused as a heavily blurred/dimmed `AmbientVideoBackdrop` (blur 24px, brightness 0.3, saturate 0.6) for motion/lighting only. All balance figures, deltas, and status labels ("BALANCE DRAINING" / "RECOVERY IN PROGRESS" / "FULLY RECOVERED") are now deterministic Remotion counters with eased drain → impact flash/shake → eased recovery, color-coded red→green. **Zero additional Higgsfield credits spent.**
- **HOOK-02 (Missed Call Counter Spiral):** Same ambient-backdrop treatment for the original Seedance footage. The "47" counter is now a deterministic spin-to-stop animation (eased 0→47, impact shake/flash at landing), and the end card is a clean `KineticText` render of "Every missed call is a missed job." with word-level highlight — fully readable, no glitches. **Zero additional Higgsfield credits spent.**
- **HOOK-03 (Chaos-to-Calm Split Screen):** Rebuilt entirely in Remotion — Higgsfield footage dropped completely. A deterministic clip-path wipe reveals a single calm "All caught up" checklist card (left) replacing six scattered, jittering notification cards (right), with a glowing divider sweep and a "VALFIN" wordmark settling at the end. This is now the brand's visual signature — an elegant, structured transformation rather than a glitch effect. **Zero additional Higgsfield credits spent** (no new generations of any kind).

All three v2 renders were produced locally via Remotion (working around a macOS<15 ffmpeg/ffprobe incompatibility with a custom binaries directory and a PCM→AAC two-pass audio pipeline), then re-validated for clean decode and balanced levels. Final files replace the original `final.mp4`/`cover.png` in place; the originals are superseded by these versions.
