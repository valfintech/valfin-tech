# Valfin Tech — Content Growth Playbook & Next Steps
**Version 1.0 | Created 2026-06-13**

> The executive layer on top of the content department: what to do first, what to build next, and what to automate over time. Read this after the [`README.md`](README.md).

## Quick Wins (do these this week — near-zero cost)

1. **Publish PKG-003 (The 7:42pm Call) and PKG-001 (They Just Call Back Faster).** They're shoot-ready, capture the brand in one line, and need no props — just a phone and good light. Start the account with the two strongest hooks.
2. **Stand up the Google Sheets content database** (schema in [`AUTOMATION_BLUEPRINT.md`](systems/AUTOMATION_BLUEPRINT.md) §3). Seed the Ideas tab from [`content_ideas.csv`](ideas/content_ideas.csv). This is your whole operating spine and it's free.
3. **UTM-tag the bio link to the Calculator** (convention in [`FILE_STRUCTURE_AND_CONVENTIONS.md`](FILE_STRUCTURE_AND_CONVENTIONS.md) §2) so you can see which content actually drives "See My Numbers" from day one. (Vercel Analytics is already live on the site.)
4. **Record PKG-009 (Why I Fixed One Real Business First) as raw founder-to-camera.** It needs no editing polish — authenticity beats production here, and it builds trust faster than any explainer.
5. **Set the weekly rhythm** from [`CONTENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`](systems/CONTENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md) §4 and commit to ~4 source videos/week, each repurposed. Consistency now beats perfection later.

## Medium-Term Opportunities (next 30–60 days)

1. **Work through all 15 production packages**, then promote the next-strongest ideas from the 100-database into packages (use the [video template](templates/VIDEO_SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md)).
2. **Turn on the repurposing system fully** — every video becomes a carousel + blog + LinkedIn post + email. This multiplies output without multiplying filming.
3. **Build the blog/resources area on the site** so blog derivatives have an evergreen home and the Calculator gets internal-link traffic.
4. **Start the weekly email** from the [email template](templates/EMAIL_NEWSLETTER_TEMPLATE.md). Even a small list compounds.
5. **Begin Phase A → B of the automation blueprint:** use Claude to draft scripts/captions/derivatives, then add n8n orchestration (C01–C03, C05, C07) reusing existing Sheets/Gmail credentials. No new paid tools yet.
6. **Run the optimization loop:** after ~3–4 weeks, rank hooks/idea-types by Calculator-visit lift and double down on what works.

## Long-Term Automation Recommendations (60+ days, on proven signal)

1. **Phase C — media generation:** add OpenAI Images + ElevenLabs VO once the content loop is proven to drive Calculator visits. Cut production time sharply.
2. **Phase D — avatar/generative video (optional):** add HeyGen/Veo only if talent-to-camera capacity becomes the bottleneck. Many strong pieces never need it.
3. **Phase E — continuous optimization:** C08 weekly digest where Claude re-prioritizes the idea backlog by real performance.
4. **Unlock proof-driven content** the moment the roofing case study lands (~60–90 days after client #1 goes live). That's the second gear: real numbers, honestly shown, will outperform everything in the pre-proof library. Plan a content sprint around it (coordinate via [`docs/CASE_STUDY_DATA_PLAN.md`](../docs/CASE_STUDY_DATA_PLAN.md)).
5. **Keep judgment human, always:** approval, any proof claim, the brand-voice gut check, and real lead conversations never get automated (see [`CONTENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`](systems/CONTENT_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md) §2).

## Recommended Next Steps for the Founder (in order)

1. **Review and approve [`docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`](../docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md).** It's the rulebook everything else obeys — make sure it sounds like you.
2. **Pick the launch 5** from the 15 packages (suggested: PKG-003, 001, 009, 005, 004 — a leak hook, the signature line, founder trust, the explainer, the Calculator driver).

5. **Publish on the weekly rhythm for 3–4 weeks. Then read the data** and let it tell you what to make more of.

## The One Thing to Remember

This content engine succeeds or fails on a single discipline: **outcomes over hype, honesty over polish.** Every package, template, and checklist here exists to keep that true at scale. When in doubt, choose the plainer, more honest line — it's almost always the on-brand one, and it's the reason Valfin will be trusted in a market full of companies that aren't.

> "Your competitors aren't better than you. They just call back faster." — and now Valfin has the content department to say so, every day, without ever sounding like an AI agency.
