## Structure

### [H1 Title]
**Opening (~100 words).** Drop the reader into the relatable scenario from the video, in prose. Make them think "that's me."

### The real problem (~150 words)
Name it and size it — honestly. Use hypotheticals clearly ("say you miss ten calls a month…"). Never an invented Valfin statistic.

### Why it happens (~150 words)
Non-judgmental, structural reasons: you're on a job, leads scatter across phone/inbox/forms, follow-up falls off after one try. Make the owner feel understood, not blamed.

### What "fixed" looks like (~200 words)
Walk the four stages in plain, outcome-first language: caught → replied in your voice → followed up over days (stops when they book) → booked on your calendar. No "AI," no "software," no "chatbot."

### The honest part (~100 words)
The proof-standard note: what we can show, what we're still measuring, the founding-partner reality where relevant. This paragraph is a feature, not a disclaimer.

### Next step (~50 words)
One CTA: the Lead Leak Calculator, framed as a one-minute, no-pitch way to see their own number. Link with UTM tags.

## SEO checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, and one subhead.
- [ ] One keyword focus — no stuffing.
- [ ] Scannable: short paragraphs, subheads, maybe one list.
- [ ] Internal links to `/calculator` and `/how-it-works`.

## Brand checklist
- [ ] No banned words. No exclamation points.
- [ ] Every number verified or clearly hypothetical.
- [ ] Outcome-first throughout; AI secondary or absent.
- [ ] One CTA.
