## 1. Caption / Hook Openers (by pillar)

### P1 — The Leak (problem awareness)
- "Your best lead this week called at [time]. Did anyone answer?"
- "You don't have a marketing problem. You have a follow-up problem."
- "The most expensive thing in your business makes no sound."
- "Here's the lead you lost last week — and never knew about."
- "Miss a few calls a month? Let's do the honest math."

### P2 — The Fix (solution education)
- "Here's what should happen in the 30 seconds after a customer reaches out."
- "If you miss a call, the customer should get a text back in seconds."
- "Caught. Replied. Followed up. Booked. That's the whole job."
- "Automatic doesn't have to mean robotic."

### P3 — Proof & Promise (trust)
- "We don't have a flashy case study yet. And we won't fake one."
- "Myth: [common belief]. Reality: [the honest correction]."
- "Here's exactly what we can prove — and what we can't yet."

### P4 — Operator's Edge (growth lessons)
- "There's one number that decides more jobs than your price."
- "You can't out-hustle a missed call at 9pm. A system can catch it."
- "Growth isn't more leads. It's less leakage."
- "Before you spend another dollar on ads — answer the leads you have."

### P5 — Founder (behind the brand)
- "Before I sold this to anyone, I built it inside one real business."
- "It hit me on a job site: this was never a marketing problem."
- "If I could tell every new owner one thing —"

## 2. CTA Lines (by funnel stage)
