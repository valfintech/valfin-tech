## Worked mini-example (from Idea #1, The 7:42pm Call)

> **Subject:** The 7:42pm call you never knew you missed
>
> Hey there,
>
> Quick one this week. Your best lead probably called after hours — say, 7:42pm — when you were finally off the clock. It went to voicemail. They didn't leave one (almost nobody does), so they called the next business on the list, and that one picked up.
>
> The hard part: you'll never see that lead in any report. It just never existed to you. That's the kind of loss that quietly adds up the most — the ones you don't even know happened.
>
> Curious what after-hours misses might be costing you? It takes about a minute to find out.
>
> **See your number →**
>
> — [Founder]

---
### Reminders
- One idea, one CTA. No hype, no exclamation points, no "AI."
- Sound like a person who's been on a job site, not a marketing list.
- Cadence: one email per week, from the week's best video.
