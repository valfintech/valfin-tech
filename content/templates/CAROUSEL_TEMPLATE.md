# Template — Carousel (5–8 slides)
**Governed by:** [`docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`](../../docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md)

> Carousels make a message saveable and swipeable. Best for "myth," "list," "how it works," and "before/after" ideas. One idea per slide. Big text, minimal words.

---

## Structure (8-slide default; collapse to 5 for simpler ideas)

| Slide | Role | Content guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | The video's hook, big and bold. Must earn the swipe. |
| 2 | **Stakes / Problem** | Name the pain or what it's costing. |
| 3 | **Body point 1** | One idea or one list item. |
| 4 | **Body point 2** | One idea or one list item. |
| 5 | **Body point 3** | One idea or one list item. |
| 6 | **Body point 4 / build** | Continue the build. |
| 7 | **Reframe / Payoff** | The "so here's the truth" turn. |
| 8 | **CTA** | One ask (Calculator or follow). |

## Design rules
- Max ~12 words per slide. Bigger is better.
- High contrast, consistent brand colors, lots of white space.
- Slide numbers or a subtle "swipe →" cue on slides 1–2.
- No banned words on any slide. No exclamation points.

## Fill-in skeleton
```
Slide 1: [HOOK]
Slide 2: [The cost / the pain]
Slide 3: [Point 1]
Slide 4: [Point 2]
Slide 5: [Point 3]
Slide 6: [Point 4 / build]
Slide 7: [The reframe]
Slide 8: [One CTA → destination]
```
