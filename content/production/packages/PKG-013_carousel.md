## Caption
"So it's basically a chatbot?" I get asked this a lot. The answer is no — and the difference really matters.

A chatbot is that little chat bubble in the corner of a website. It answers questions for someone who's already on your page. Useful, but narrow.

This is a different thing entirely. It catches phone calls. It catches texts. It catches form fills. All the ways a real customer actually tries to reach you — including when nobody's anywhere near your website. And it doesn't stop after one reply: it follows up over the next several days until the customer books or clearly says no.

It's not a chat bubble bolted to your site. It's closer to your whole front desk — answering, following up, and booking, around the clock. See how it works, link in bio.
