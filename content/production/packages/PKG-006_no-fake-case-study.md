## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
We don't have a published case study yet. And we won't fabricate one to look further along than we are.

In the automation and "AI" space, invented proof is the norm — "300% more leads," "10x growth," no client named, no methodology shown. It's so common that real honesty now stands out.

So here's where we actually are: we built our system inside one real roofing business. It's live and running their lead pipeline today. We're in the middle of measuring its impact — properly, with their real records — and when we can show a number, we'll show the math behind it, not just the headline.

That's a slower path to proof. We're choosing it on purpose. The day we publish results, every one of them will be real — which means we'll never have to walk anything back.

Trust earned beats trust borrowed.
