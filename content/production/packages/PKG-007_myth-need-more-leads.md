## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
"I need more leads" is the most common thing I hear from local business owners. It's almost always the wrong diagnosis.

Most owner-operated businesses don't have a lead-volume problem. They have a lead-retention problem. The leads come in — and then most get one contact attempt before everyone moves on to the next thing.

But buyers rarely act on the first touch. The bookings live in the second, third, and fourth follow-up that, realistically, a busy owner never gets to.

So before spending more to pour additional leads into the top of the funnel, the higher-ROI move is almost always to make sure the leads you already pay for get followed up — consistently, automatically, until they book or clearly decline.

Keep what you've got before you buy more.
