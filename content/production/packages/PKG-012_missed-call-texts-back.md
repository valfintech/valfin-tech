## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
A simple capability that quietly saves jobs: if a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text back within seconds — automatically, in the business's own voice.

Consider the typical scenario for a trade business. The owner is on a job, hands full, and a call comes in. They genuinely cannot answer. Under normal circumstances, that lead is lost — and usually invisibly, because no one ever knows it was there.

Now change one thing. The moment the call is missed, the caller receives: "Sorry we missed you — this is [Business]. What can we help with?" The conversation continues by text, on the customer's terms, and the owner picks it up when they're down off the ladder.

It's not flashy. It's not complicated to the customer. But the difference between "missed call, gone forever" and "missed call, conversation started" is, over a year, a lot of recovered revenue.
