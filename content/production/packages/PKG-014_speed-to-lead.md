## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
For decades, word of mouth was the growth engine for local service businesses. It still matters. But a quieter force is increasingly deciding who wins the job: speed-to-lead.

Speed-to-lead is simply how fast a business responds after a customer reaches out. What's changed isn't the metric — it's the expectation around it. Customers now live in an instant-response world. Instant answers, instant confirmations, instant delivery. Against that backdrop, a business that takes a day to return a call doesn't read as "busy and in-demand." It reads as unreliable.

The practical consequence: businesses that respond within seconds — including after hours, when intent is often highest — are steadily winning work from equally-skilled competitors who respond slowly. The quality of the work isn't the variable. The speed of the first response is.

For any lead-driven business, this is the shift worth getting ahead of. The advantage is still available precisely because most haven't acted on it yet.

Which side of it is your business on?
