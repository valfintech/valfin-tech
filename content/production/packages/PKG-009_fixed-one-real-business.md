## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
One of our core principles is "build in the field, not in a lab." Here's what that actually meant in practice.

Before we called this a product, we ran the entire system inside one real roofing company — their live phones, their real leads, their actual customers. Not a sandbox, not a demo environment.

We did that on purpose. I didn't want a future client to be the one who discovered the edge cases. I wanted to hit them ourselves first. And we did — the kind of issues that only surface when a real customer replies at an odd hour, or a date gets entered in a way the system didn't expect. We fixed them in the field, against reality.

The result is that when we say it works, it's not a projection. It's an observation from months of running an actual business.

That's a slower, more honest way to build. It's the only way I'd want to sell something I expect a business to rely on.
