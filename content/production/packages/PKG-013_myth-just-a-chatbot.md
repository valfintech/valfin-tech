## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
"Is it basically a chatbot?" It's the most common question we get, and the answer is no. The distinction is worth being precise about.

A chatbot lives in a chat bubble on a website and answers questions for someone who's already there. It's a single channel, and only for people who've already found and opened your site.

What we run covers the channels where local-service revenue actually comes in: phone calls, text messages, and form submissions — including all the times nobody is on your website at all. And it doesn't stop at a single automated reply. It follows up over several days until the customer books or declines.

The better mental model isn't "a bot on your site." It's "your front desk" — capturing every inbound contact, responding fast in your voice, following up, and booking — without depending on someone being free to do it.

Getting this distinction right matters, because "chatbot" dramatically undersells what reliable response infrastructure actually does for a business.
