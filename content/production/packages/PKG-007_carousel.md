## Caption
Myth: "I need more leads."
Reality: most owners don't. They need to stop losing the ones they already have.

Quick gut check — how many leads from last month did you actually follow up with more than once? If you're like most of us: you called once, heard nothing, and moved on to the next fire.

Here's the catch. Most people don't book on the first try. The jobs are sitting in the follow-up nobody does — the second text, the day-3 check-in, the "still want that quote?" message.
