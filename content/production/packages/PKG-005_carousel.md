## Caption
Here's what should happen in the 30 seconds after a customer reaches out — even if you never touch your phone:

1. It gets caught. Call, text, or form — nothing slips through.
2. They get a real, friendly reply, in your voice (not a robot's).
3. If they don't book right away, it follows up for you — day one, day three, day seven — and stops the second they book or say no. No nagging.
4. The ones who are ready end up on your calendar.

All of it runs whether you're on a roof or fast asleep. That's the difference between "I hope I caught everyone" and "nothing gets missed."
