## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
Customers rarely hire the best business. They hire the first one that makes them feel taken care of.

It's worth sitting with that, because it runs counter to how most owners think about competition. We assume the decision is rational: quality, price, reviews. But for urgent, stressful services — a leak, no heat, a burst pipe — the customer isn't in a comparison mindset. They're in a relief mindset.

The first business to respond and convey "we've got you" tends to win, often before price or reviews enter the picture at all. Responsiveness isn't a customer-service nicety in these moments; it's the actual moment of sale.

Which means the highest-leverage improvement for many service businesses isn't being better. It's being first to reply — reliably, every time, including the nights and weekends when stress peaks.
