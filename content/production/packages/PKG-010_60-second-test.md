## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
A 60-second exercise every owner-operator should run on their own business — and most are quietly afraid to.

Call your own business from a number it doesn't recognize, as if you were a brand-new customer. Count the rings. Notice whether anyone answers, and what happens if they don't. Then text the same number with a simple question and time the reply.

Whatever you just experienced is what your customers experience every day. Not the version you imagine — the real one.

It's uncomfortable, which is exactly why it's useful. You can't improve an intake experience you've never actually felt from the outside. Run the test before you spend another dollar trying to get more people to go through it.
