## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
Some quick, honest math (illustrative — not a claimed result):

Say a busy crew misses 10 calls a month. Suppose 3 of those would have become jobs. If an average job is worth $2,000, that's $6,000 a month leaving the business — quietly, unmeasured, every month.

And that's before follow-up: the leads who don't book on the first contact and never get a second one.

I share the example not because the number is universal — it isn't — but because most owners have never actually done this calculation for their own business. When they do, it reframes the whole conversation. The question stops being "how do I get more leads?" and becomes "how much am I already losing, and what would it cost to stop?"

The honest version uses your real numbers. Worth the sixty seconds to find out.
