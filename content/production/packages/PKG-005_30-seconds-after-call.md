## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
What should actually happen in the first 30 seconds after a customer reaches out?

For most lead-driven businesses, the honest answer is "it depends on whether someone's free." That's the gap where revenue leaks.

Here's the version that doesn't depend on you being available:

→ Captured. Every call, text, and form lands in one place.
→ Responded. The customer gets a real, professional reply in the business's own voice, within seconds.
→ Followed up. If they don't book immediately, a short sequence follows up over the next week — and stops the moment they book or decline.
→ Booked. The ready ones end up on the calendar.

None of it requires the owner to drop what they're doing. It's the difference between hoping you caught everyone and knowing nothing slipped through — and it runs nights, weekends, and holidays included.
