## 1. Video Objective
A pure awareness hook. Make after-hours lead loss feel specific and real with a single, concrete moment — the 7:42pm call — so an owner instantly recognizes themselves and follows.

## 3. Opening Hook
"Your best lead this week called at 7:42pm." (Specific time matters — it feels real, not generic.)

## 4. Voiceover Script
Same as script. Tone: calm, almost a quiet ghost story. No blame ("fair enough"). Let "it just… never existed to you" sit for a beat.

## 12. LinkedIn Adaptation
Your best lead this week probably called at 7:42pm.

Not during business hours. Not when someone was sitting by the phone. After dinner, on a weeknight, when the owner was finally off the clock.

It went to voicemail. They didn't leave one — almost nobody does anymore. So they called the next business on the list, and that one answered.

The hardest part of this kind of loss is that it's invisible. It never shows up in a report. The owner never knows it happened. You can't fix a leak you can't see.

For lead-driven local businesses, the after-hours window is often where the most revenue quietly slips away — precisely because no one's measuring it. The first step is making that invisible loss visible.
