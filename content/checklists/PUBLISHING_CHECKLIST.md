# Publishing Checklist
**Run at the moment of scheduling/publishing, after the [Content Approval Checklist](CONTENT_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md) has passed.**

---

## Before Scheduling
- [ ] Content approval checklist = **Approved** (human sign-off recorded).
- [ ] Final master video is the correct version (`vN`) and 9:16, captions burned in.
- [ ] Watched **on mute** — hook lands in first 2 seconds, message is clear without sound.
- [ ] Bio link points to the **current campaign destination** (default: Calculator).
- [ ] All links UTM-tagged (`utm_content = PKG-0XX`).

## Scheduling
- [ ] Posted to / queued on all target platforms (IG, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn as applicable).
- [ ] **Spacing respected** — not all derivatives the same hour (video Day 0; carousel +2; blog +3; email week-end).
- [ ] Posting time matches platform best windows for a US local-business audience.
- [ ] Native upload per platform (no foreign watermarks).

## At Publish
- [ ] Live links verified working (tap the bio link; confirm Calculator/contact loads).
- [ ] Founder/operator available for **first 60–90 min** of community management.
- [ ] Any incoming DM that looks like a real lead → routed into the **sales process** (internal lead pipeline), not treated as a comment.

## After Publish (within 7 days)
- [ ] Performance logged in the tracker: views, **saves, shares, Calculator visits, contact submits**, 3s retention.
- [ ] Derivatives (carousel/blog/email) all published and logged.
- [ ] Note one learning (what worked / what didn't) for the weekly optimization review.

## Weekly
- [ ] Review performance; rank hooks/idea-types by **Calculator-visit lift** (not likes).
- [ ] Feed learnings back into idea prioritization (Stage 10 → Stage 1).
- [ ] Update strategy/guidelines if a real pattern emerged (living documents).
