# Content Approval Checklist
**Run this on EVERY piece of content — source videos AND every derivative — before it publishes.**
**Source of truth:** [`docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md`](../../docs/CONTENT_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md)

> If any box fails, the piece does not publish until fixed. No exceptions on the honesty and banned-word gates.

---

## A. Positioning & Message
- [ ] Leads with a **pain or outcome the owner feels** — not with technology.
- [ ] **AI is secondary** (or absent). If mentioned, it comes after the benefit and is framed as one part of a system.
- [ ] Does **not** promise or imply **lead generation** / "more leads."
- [ ] Could **not** be mistaken for selling a website **chatbot**.
- [ ] Maps cleanly to **one content pillar** (P1–P5).
- [ ] Reinforces (or is consistent with) "this was never a marketing problem."

## B. Honesty / Proof Standard
- [ ] Every number is **either** a verified real client figure **or** clearly labeled as a hypothetical example / "in progress."
- [ ] **No invented statistics, no fake case studies, no "typical results."**
- [ ] No hedging language ("results may vary").
- [ ] If a claim was uncertain, the **more honest** version was chosen (or the claim was cut).

## C. Banned Words (hard fail)
Scan for and confirm NONE present:
- [ ] "AI Employee" (as identity) · "chatbot" / "bot"* · "software" / "SaaS" / "app" / "tool" / "platform"
- [ ] "revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "cutting-edge" / "next-gen" / "unlock" / "supercharge" / "10x" / "skyrocket"
- [ ] Exclamation points
- [ ] Any published price / dollar amount presented as our pricing

*\*Exception: PKG-013 uses "chatbot" only to bust the myth and separate us from it. Nowhere else.*

## D. Clarity & Voice
- [ ] **Five-second clarity** — hook and main claim understandable in one read.
- [ ] **~8th-grade reading level** — short sentences, plain words, business-owner vocabulary.
- [ ] Tone is **calm, honest, practical, premium-but-approachable** — no shouting, no hype.
- [ ] Sounds like **a real person**, not a brand or an AI agency.

## E. Structure & CTA
- [ ] **Exactly one CTA**, matched to the funnel stage (Awareness=follow, Consideration=Calculator, Conversion=message us).
- [ ] CTA destination works (Calculator / contact / profile).
- [ ] On-screen text (if video): 3–6 words at a time, high-contrast, **no banned words on screen**.

## F. Platform Fit
- [ ] Caption/description rewritten **native** to the platform (LinkedIn ≠ TikTok voice).
- [ ] No cross-platform watermark (e.g., no IG logo on a TikTok upload).
- [ ] Hashtags appropriate to platform count + audience.

## G. Tracking
- [ ] Link is **UTM-tagged** per conventions.
- [ ] Tracker row created/updated with `POST-` ID, pillar, funnel stage, and source `PKG-`.
